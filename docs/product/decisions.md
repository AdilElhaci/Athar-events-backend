# Decisions

**Status: recorded owner decisions.** A decision here is binding for planning and implementation. A row is never rewritten: a later decision supersedes an earlier one and both stay, with the earlier one struck through. ADR-level decisions also have an ADR; the ADR stays `Proposed` until the owner accepts it ([D-21](#d-21)).

Origin values: **Owner** (the owner decided), **Owner-approved review** (recommended by the Claude–Codex review and approved by the owner on 2026-10-24 as part of the planning proposal), **Default pending client** (the owner approved it as the recommended default; the client still has to confirm the business rule in the linked open question).
